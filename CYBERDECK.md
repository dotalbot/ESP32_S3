# Pocket cyberdeck

The larger Freenove **3.5-inch FNK0104N** now has its own [board guide](main/boards/dominic-fnk0104n-cyberdeck/README.md) and build profile, `dominic-fnk0104n-cyberdeck`. It reuses the apps with a 480 × 320 layout, larger Home controls and calibrated battery voltage / estimated percentage. The original board's profile and pins remain separate. Current larger-board validation and remaining hardware checks are recorded in that guide; the entries below describe the original 2.8-inch development history.

Open [the board guide](main/boards/dominic-es3c28p-cyberdeck/README.md) for the menu, build command, hardware assumptions and first USB test.
